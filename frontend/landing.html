View FAQs

What type of occasion do I use DONO for?

Any occasion you can think of! Birthdays, holidays, wedding registries, baby registries, anniversaries, charity drives, or even general shopping lists.

Can I make my wishlist private?

Yes! You can choose your privacy settings when creating a wishlist, and update them anytime later.

Can my friends see what I claim?

No way! Claiming is completely anonymous. Click “Claim” to reserve a gift so no one else buys it, and on your own list you never see what’s been claimed.
